# Course Feedback

A quick note before the semester ends — I'd really appreciate your feedback on IGME-340. There are two places to leave it, and both matter for different reasons.

## RIT SmartEvals (Official)

**Link:** https://rit.smartevals.com/

This is the university's official course evaluation. Two things worth knowing about how it works:

- **The quantitative scores** (the numeric ratings) are what administration weights most heavily when evaluating the course and the instructor. If the course went well for you and we connected during the semester, letting that show in the numbers genuinely helps.
- **The written comments** are what I actually read when I'm planning changes for next semester. That's where you can tell me what worked, what didn't, and what to try differently.

Please be honest in both. The numbers matter to the institution; the comments matter to me.

## Anonymous Google Form (For Me Only)

**Link:** https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSekhbQIl3sZkdhHlce3SM90ddBNosOWtFCmgFf9BPSkuzU8hw/viewform

SmartEvals is structured to work for every course at RIT, which means it can miss course-specific stuff. This form is a single open-ended question for feedback that would actually help me improve IGME-340:

> **What's one or two things you'd change about this course to make it better, and why?**

Anonymous, separate from your grade, goes only to me.
