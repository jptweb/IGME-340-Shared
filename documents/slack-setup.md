We use Slack for course communication, quick questions, and announcements. It is not a graded assignment, but it is where I answer things fastest, so get in early.

### RIT Enterprise Slack Login

RIT uses Enterprise Slack with Single Sign-On (SSO). When logging in:

1. **Don't enter your password on Slack's login page**
2. Click **"Sign in with SSO"** or look for the RIT authentication option
3. You'll be redirected to RIT's login system
4. Use your regular RIT credentials there

This is different from a personal Slack account - your RIT login works across all RIT Slack workspaces.

## Two Things To Do Once You Are In

Neither is graded. Both make the semester easier.

### 1. Introduce Yourself

In **#340-general**, find my welcome/introduction post and **reply in the thread** with a brief intro:
- Your name and preferred name/pronouns if you'd like
- Your major/year
- Something interesting (a hobby, what you're working on, a fun fact)

**Important:** Reply in the thread, don't create a new post - this keeps the channel organized.

### 2. Add a Profile Picture

Help us associate a face (or avatar) with your name (i'll make this optional but it really helps):

1. Click your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **"Edit Profile"**
3. Upload an image

A photo of you is preferred, but an avatar works too. Just don't impersonate others or violate RIT's Code of Conduct.

## Why Slack Instead of Discord?

You might wonder why we don't use Discord. A few reasons:

- **Industry standard:** Slack is widely used in professional settings - good to be familiar with it
- **Security:** Enterprise Slack meets RIT's privacy/security requirements for education
- **Work/life separation:** Keeping school communication separate from gaming/social platforms helps maintain boundaries
- **Threaded discussions:** Great for keeping conversations organized

## Tips for Managing Slack

### Use Threads
When replying to someone's post, click **"Reply in thread"** instead of posting to the channel. This keeps discussions organized and reduces notification noise.

### Customize Notifications
Don't want constant pings? Go to **Preferences → Notifications** and adjust what triggers alerts. You can mute channels, set quiet hours, or only get notified for direct mentions.

### Desktop vs Mobile
The website works great on desktop - no need to install anything. There's a desktop app too if you prefer that.

The phone app is worth having for notifications when you're away from your computer, but **signing in on a phone is not obvious and the Google button will not get you in.** Use the workspace URL path:

1. Install Slack from the App Store or Google Play and open it
2. Choose **Sign in with workspace URL**. On iOS it's hiding under "Having trouble?" on the email screen
3. Enter `rit-community` as the workspace URL
4. Tap **Sign In with RIT**, then use your normal RIT username and password

`rit-community` is the workspace that hands the app off to RIT authentication. Once you're through it, you're signed in to every RIT workspace you belong to, this course included.

**Already have Slack on your phone** for another RIT workspace? Swipe right for the channels menu, tap the workspace icon at the top left, then **Add a workspace → Sign in to another workspace**, and pick up from step 2.

RIT's official version of these steps is [in the ITS knowledge base](https://help.rit.edu/sp?id=kb_article&table=kb_knowledge&sysparm_article=KB0043536).
